Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Olly’s Baked Assortments (“OBA Cookies,” “OBA,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) may collect through our website and services, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Effective: October 1, 2026La Mesa, California
Current-feature policy: This policy describes OBA’s current website, ordering, support, analytics, and Cookie Crew functionality. Planned Longneck customer-account features are not treated as active until they launch.

1. Information You Provide

We may receive information you choose to provide when you place an order, request pickup or delivery, contact OBA, submit a custom-order or event inquiry, or use an authorized Cookie Crew account.

  • Contact information such as your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Order and fulfillment information, including selected products, pickup details, delivery address, delivery notes, and order notes.
  • Inquiry information you include in emails or contact-form requests, including custom-order, market, pop-up, event, collaboration, and support details.
  • Authorized Cookie Crew account information used for staff authentication and password recovery.

2. Payment Information

OBA’s website uses Square for secure payment processing. Payment-card fields are provided through Square’s payment technology. OBA’s website receives payment tokens and transaction results needed to complete an order rather than storing your full payment-card number in the website’s own cart code.

Square’s handling of payment information is governed by Square’s own privacy practices and terms.

3. Information Collected Automatically

Our website may collect basic technical and usage information when you visit, such as browser/device information, pages viewed, referring information, and interactions with the site. OBA currently uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic and performance.

The website also uses browser storage for functional purposes. For example, cart information is stored on your device so Your Batch can persist while you browse, and checkout may use temporary session storage to support a checkout attempt.

4. How We Use Information

  • Process, prepare, fulfill, and support orders.
  • Calculate or confirm eligible fulfillment options such as local delivery.
  • Respond to questions, Custom Feast inquiries, event requests, collaborations, and other messages.
  • Operate and secure authorized Cookie Crew tools, including authentication and password recovery.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, measure, and improve the OBA website and customer experience.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud, or security incidents and comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Service Providers and Third Parties

We may use service providers that process information as necessary to provide website and business functions. Current website functionality includes services associated with payment processing, website analytics, maps/address functionality, hosting/infrastructure, and email. These providers may process information under their own privacy terms.

We do not state that OBA sells personal information. If OBA’s practices materially change, this policy should be updated before those changes are represented here.

6. Contact and Inquiry Messages

Messages sent to OBA may be delivered to ollysbakedassortments@gmail.com. Information in an inquiry is used to understand and respond to the request and, when relevant, to coordinate an order, event, pop-up, collaboration, or support issue.

7. Cookie Crew Accounts

Cookie Crew access is restricted to authorized OBA staff. Crew tools use account credentials and may process account identity, role information, sessions, and password-reset requests. Password-reset links are intended only for the authorized account holder.

Longneck customer accounts are still planned. Before customer accounts launch, OBA intends to update its account documentation and this Privacy Policy as needed to describe the information those accounts store and how it is used.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including order fulfillment, customer support, business records, security, dispute resolution, and applicable legal or accounting requirements. Retention periods may differ by type of information and service provider.

9. Security

OBA uses reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to a small online business, including server-side processing for sensitive operations and third-party payment technology. No website, email system, or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact OBA to ask questions about personal information you have provided, request corrections where appropriate, or ask about deletion of information that OBA is able and permitted to delete. Some records may need to be retained for transactions, legal obligations, security, or legitimate business purposes.

Contact privacy requests at ollysbakedassortments@gmail.com or through our Contact page.

11. Children’s Privacy

The OBA website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to OBA without appropriate permission, contact us so we can review the request.

12. External Links

The website may link to third-party services, social platforms, maps, or other websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this Privacy Policy.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as OBA’s website and services change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current published version.
