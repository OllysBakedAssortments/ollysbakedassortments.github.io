Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know before your first order. Don't see your question here? Just email us.

How do I place an order?

Right now, orders go through our Hotplate shop. Browse this week's flavors, add your cookies or gift box, and check out there. We're working on bringing ordering directly to this website soon!

Where do I pick up my order, and is delivery available?

Pickup details and any delivery options are listed for each order on Hotplate, since they can vary week to week. Check your order confirmation for the specifics, or reach out if you have questions.

How far in advance should I order?

Our flavors rotate every two weeks and quantities are limited, since everything is baked in small batches. We recommend ordering as soon as preorders open on Hotplate to make sure your favorites don't sell out.

Do you accommodate allergies or dietary restrictions?

Our cookies are baked in a home kitchen and typically contain common allergens like gluten, dairy, and eggs. If you have allergies or dietary concerns, please reach out to us before ordering at info@ollysbakedassortments.com so we can answer your specific questions.

Are the Safari Snacks safe for my dog?

Safari Snacks are made with dog-friendly ingredients like peanut butter and banana. Every pup is different, so if your dog has food sensitivities, check with your vet or reach out to us with any questions before treating them.

Can I request a custom flavor or order for an event?

We'd love to hear your idea! Email us at info@ollysbakedassortments.com with details on your event and what you're picturing, and we'll let you know what's possible.

Where can I find Olly's Baked Assortments in person?

We pop up at farmers markets and street fairs around East County San Diego. Visit the Events page for the current schedule.

What sizes of gift boxes do you offer?

We offer three sizes — the Lil Giraffe Pack (3 cookies), Tall Giraffe Pack (6 cookies), and Giraffe Feast (13 cookies). You choose which cookies go in from that week's flavor lineup. See the Gift Boxes page for details.

Still Have Questions?

Send us a message and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
