🍪 Ordering & Pricing Ordering, Giraffe Packs, pricing, checkout and payment
How do I order OBA Cookies?

Order directly through the OBA Cookies website. Choose from the cookies currently available, add them to Your Batch, and continue to checkout to select your available fulfillment option and complete payment.

Order OBA Cookies →
What is a Giraffe Pack?

A Giraffe Pack is OBA’s build-your-own cookie box. Choose from the available flavors to put together a pack of cookies for yourself, to share, or to give as a gift.

Explore Giraffe Packs →
What Giraffe Pack sizes are available?

OBA currently offers three sizes: the Lil Giraffe Pack with 3 cookies, Tall Giraffe Pack with 6 cookies, and Giraffe Feast with 13 cookies.

Explore Giraffe Packs →
Can I choose the cookies in my Giraffe Pack?

Yes. Giraffe Packs are designed so you can choose from the cookie flavors available for that week. Availability still depends on the current inventory of each flavor.

Explore Giraffe Packs →
How much do OBA Cookies cost?

Pricing depends on how you get your cookies, the type of cookie, and the pack or order you choose. Bertha, online ordering, Giraffe Packs, specialty cookies, custom orders, and events can have different pricing. Visit Pricing & Ordering for the current breakdown.

Pricing & Ordering →
Why can prices be different between Bertha, online orders, and events?

OBA uses different pricing for different ways of getting cookies. Bertha serves the neighborhood through Grab-N-Go, while online orders reserve inventory ahead of pickup or delivery, and markets and events have their own operating costs. The Pricing & Ordering page explains the current prices for each option.

Pricing & Ordering →
Why is there a timer during checkout?

When cookies are reserved for your checkout, they are temporarily held so another customer can’t purchase the same inventory while you complete your order. The timer shows how long that reservation remains active.

What happens if my checkout timer expires?

If the reservation expires before checkout is completed, the reserved cookies can be released back into available inventory. You may need to rebuild or update your batch before checking out.

Can a cookie sell out while I’m ordering?

Yes. OBA bakes in limited batches, so flavors can sell out. The reservation system is designed to temporarily hold available cookies once they enter the checkout reservation process, but availability is not final until the website confirms it.

What payment methods does OBA accept online?

OBA accepts supported credit and debit cards through our online checkout, which is processed securely by Square. Any additional payment options available for your device will appear during checkout.

When can I pick up my order?

Preordering opens Monday at 12 PM and closes Friday at 9 PM. Orders are ready for pickup beginning Saturday, with pickup available Saturday from 2–8 PM, Sunday from 11 AM–8 PM, and Monday from 11 AM–8 PM. Choose your available pickup date during checkout.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes. Preorders may be cancelled for a full refund before preorders close at 9 PM on Friday. 🦒 Bertha The Friendly Neighborhood Cookie Cart, hours and Grab-N-Go
Who—or what—is Bertha?

Bertha is OBA’s Friendly Neighborhood Cookie Cart—the cart where OBA’s neighborhood Grab-N-Go began and where Longnecks can stop by for cookies when she’s open.

When is Bertha open?

Bertha’s regular neighborhood schedule is Saturday at 3 PM through Tuesday at 8 PM, subject to availability and schedule changes.

Where is Bertha?

Bertha is OBA's neighborhood cookie cart in La Mesa, California. Find Olly's Baked Assortments on Google Maps or Apple Maps below.

Google Maps ↗ Apple Maps ↗
How does Bertha Grab-N-Go work?

Stop by while Bertha is open, see what cookies are available, and grab what you’d like. Bertha inventory is first come, first served, so flavors and quantities are not guaranteed.

Can cookies sell out at Bertha?

Yes. Bertha carries limited quantities, and Grab-N-Go cookies are first come, first served. A flavor can sell out before Bertha’s scheduled closing time.

Does Bertha always have the same flavors as the website?

Bertha will typically have the flavors advertised online while supplies last, but her selection can vary as flavors sell out. She may also have surprise extras that aren't listed online, including additional flavors, brownies, and other treats for the neighborhood and community.

Can I reserve cookies from Bertha?

Bertha’s Grab-N-Go selection itself is first come, first served. If you want cookies reserved ahead of time, use OBA’s online ordering system for the available preorder/pickup options rather than relying on Bertha inventory.

Order OBA Cookies →
Why is Bertha pricing different?

Bertha uses special neighborhood Grab-N-Go pricing. Online orders and other sales channels can have different prices because they involve different ordering and fulfillment processes. See Pricing & Ordering for the current breakdown.

🥛 Cookies & Allergens Ingredients, allergens, storage and dietary information
What allergens are in OBA Cookies?

OBA Cookies are made in a home kitchen where common allergens are used, including wheat/gluten, dairy, and eggs. Individual flavors may contain additional allergens, such as peanuts or tree nuts, depending on their ingredients. Check the ingredient and allergen information provided for each product online and on its packaging.

Can OBA accommodate food allergies?

OBA Cookies are prepared in a home kitchen where multiple ingredients and allergens are handled, so we cannot guarantee that products are free from cross-contact with allergens. If you have a food allergy or allergen question, review the ingredient and allergen information for the specific product and contact OBA before ordering if you need additional information.

Contact OBA →
Is cross-contact possible?

Yes. OBA Cookies are prepared in a home kitchen where multiple ingredients and allergens are handled, so cross-contact is possible. We maintain a clean work environment and routinely change gloves between tasks, but we cannot guarantee that any product is completely free from cross-contact with allergens.

Where are OBA Cookies baked?

OBA is a home-based cottage bakery in La Mesa, California, baking cookies in small batches.

About OBA →
Where can I find ingredients and allergen information for a specific cookie?

Ingredient and allergen information for OBA products is available on the website and on individual product packaging. Check the information for the specific cookie or treat you're interested in, since ingredients and allergens can vary by product.

See OBA Flavors →
How often do OBA flavors change?

OBA's cookie lineup typically rotates on a roughly two-week cycle. Seasonal cookies, specials, and occasional surprise treats may also appear outside the regular rotation.

See OBA Flavors →
How should I store my OBA Cookies?

For the best freshness, keep your cookies in a sealed container or their packaging. OBA Cookies can be stored at room temperature for about 3–5 days, refrigerated for up to 1–2 weeks, or frozen in a tightly sealed freezer bag or container for up to 3 months. For a warm, fresh-baked feel, reheat your cookie briefly in the microwave before enjoying.

What are Safari Snacks?

Safari Snacks are OBA’s treats made specifically for dogs. They’re made with a simple combination of oats, peanuts, banana, and eggs, and are separate from OBA’s regular cookie lineup for people.

Does OBA offer gluten-free, vegan, or other dietary options?

OBA does not currently offer a regular gluten-free or vegan cookie option. As we expand in flavors and ingredients, gluten-free, vegan, or other dietary options may become available. Check the current menu and individual product information for the latest available options and ingredients.
