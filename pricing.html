Preorder & Pickup

Preorders are baked and reserved specifically for you, so you don’t have to worry about your favorites being gone when you arrive.

Why is preorder pricing different from Bertha?

Bertha’s $2–$3 pricing is our Neighborhood Thank-You pricing and is based on whatever is currently available Grab-N-Go. A preorder guarantees your requested flavors and quantities and reserves that batch specifically for you.
