Quick Prices

The short version.

OBA uses different pricing by sales channel. Standard and Specialty are the two main full-size cookie price tiers; seasonal, classic, and fan-favorite labels describe the flavor lineup rather than automatically changing the price.

Bertha

Neighborhood Grab-N-Go

$3 / $4

$3 standard and $4 Specialty. Bertha’s neighborhood inventory is first come, first served.

Online

Order Ahead

$4 / $5

$4 standard and $5 Specialty. Online ordering reserves available cookies for pickup or another eligible fulfillment option.

Markets & Events

Event-Day Pricing

$4–$6

Most cookies are generally $4–$5 at markets and events, with select cookies priced up to $6 depending on the bake and event.

Standard cookies include OBA classics, classic favorites, fan favorites, and many rotating seasonal flavors. Specialty cookies are select premium or more involved bakes, often featuring stuffed centers, premium ingredients, extra toppings, or layered components.

Seasonal doesn’t automatically mean Specialty. “Seasonal” describes when a flavor appears; “Specialty” is a pricing category.

Online Ordering

Reserved, freshly prepared, and ready for you.

Online ordering is the planned-ahead OBA experience: choose available weekly flavors, reserve the quantities you want, and let us prepare your order for pickup instead of relying on whatever remains in Bertha.

Why preorder? You are not only reserving inventory. Preorders are baked the day of pickup, with batches scheduled as close to the pickup window as our bake schedule allows so your order can be prepared as fresh as possible.

Common Questions

A few pricing questions, answered.

For ordering, pickup, allergens, Bertha, accounts, and other customer questions, visit the full OBA Help Center.

Are seasonal cookies automatically Specialty cookies?

No. Seasonal describes when a flavor is available. Specialty is a pricing category. Many seasonal flavors use standard pricing, while select premium or more involved bakes are classified as Specialty.

Why is Bertha less expensive than online ordering?

Bertha uses special neighborhood Grab-N-Go pricing with limited, first-come, first-served inventory. Online orders reserve available flavors and quantities in advance and include the planned preorder experience: day-of-pickup baking scheduled as close to pickup as our bake schedule allows, prepared packaging with ribbon and an OBA cookie/giraffe sticker pack, and alphabetically organized pickup.

Can I put Specialty cookies in a Giraffe Pack?

Yes. Specialty cookies can be selected in a Giraffe Pack for +$1 per Specialty cookie, subject to the flavors available that week.

How much does a Custom Feast cost?

Custom Feasts start at $45 for 6–12 custom cookies with up to 3 premium or specialty ingredients. Additional premium ingredients are +$2.50 each, and each cookie beyond 12 is +$2.50.

Are farmers market and event prices always the same?

No. Most cookies are generally $4–$5 at markets and events, while select cookies may be priced up to $6 depending on the bake and event.
