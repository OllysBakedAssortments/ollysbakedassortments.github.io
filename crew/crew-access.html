ROLE MODEL

Default Access Levels

Roles provide the baseline for Crew and Managers. Owner / Admin accounts retain full system access.

Crew Day-to-day operational access. Can perform assigned work but cannot administer staff or elevated business controls.
Manager Inherits Crew access plus broader operational authority. Selected elevated capabilities can be granted individually.
Owner / Admin Full administrative authority, including roles, capability overrides, invitations, security recovery, and protected settings.
