    Pricing & Ordering Guide

    Choose the Cookie Route That Fits

    OBA Cookies can be ordered a few different ways. Pricing and availability depend on whether you are ordering the current weekly lineup, building a Giraffe Pack, stopping by Bertha, requesting something custom, visiting us at a market, or choosing local delivery.

    Order Ahead

    📦 Preorder & Pickup

    Choose from the current weekly lineup and reserve your cookies before pickup. The live prices shown on each cookie card above are the prices used for this order.

    Best for:

    Getting the flavors and quantities you want before they sell out.

    Neighborhood Stop

    🍪 Bertha Grab-N-Go

    Bertha is OBA’s Friendly Neighborhood Cookie Cart. Grab-N-Go cookies are first come, first served, so the available flavors and quantities can change throughout the weekend.

    Best for:

    Spontaneous neighborhood cookie runs and seeing what Bertha has stocked.

    Made For You

    ✨ Custom Cookie Orders

    Custom orders start with an OBA pack and may include a custom-order fee plus additional charges for premium ingredients or special additions.

    Best for:

    Celebrations, gifts, special flavor ideas, and larger planned orders.

    🦒 Bertha’s Neighborhood Thank-You

    OBA started in the neighborhood with a small cookie stand and neighbors stopping by for something sweet. Bertha keeps that neighborhood tradition going with first-come, first-served Grab-N-Go cookies.

    Because Bertha’s stock changes as cookies sell, preorder above when you want a specific flavor or quantity reserved for you.

    🎪 Farmers Markets & Events

    OBA also appears at farmers markets, pop-ups, street fairs, and community events around East County San Diego. Event menus and pricing can differ from neighborhood or preorder pricing because the operating setup is different.

    See markets & events →

    🚗 Local Delivery

    Delivery is available only when the address qualifies. Enter the delivery address in the fulfillment step above to see whether the order is eligible and to receive the current delivery quote before payment.

    💡 Why Can Prices Vary?

    Each ordering route has a different mix of availability, reservation, customization, packaging, fulfillment, event operations, and delivery costs. The cart and checkout total on this page are the controlling prices for an online order placed here.

    Online order total: Cookie prices, pack adjustments, delivery charges, taxes, and the final amount due are shown in the live order summary and checkout before payment is submitted.
