Delivery & Shipping FAQ

A few quick answers about getting OBA beyond our regular La Mesa pickup.

Where can OBA Cookies be shipped?

Shipping is currently limited to eligible addresses within California.

What is the minimum order for shipping?

Shipping begins with the Tall Giraffe Pack: 6 cookies for $16 before applicable shipping charges or specialty-cookie upgrades.

What is the minimum order for local delivery?

Local delivery also begins at 6 cookies. Delivery is intended for qualifying addresses within approximately 15 miles of OBA Cookies in La Mesa.

Can I choose my cookie flavors?

Yes. Available flavor choices will depend on the current OBA lineup and which flavors are available for the selected fulfillment method.

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping is separate from the listed Giraffe Pack price. The applicable shipping rate will be shown or confirmed before your order is finalized.

Can I send OBA Cookies as a gift?

Absolutely. Giraffe Packs are a natural fit for birthdays, thank-yous, celebrations, care packages, and sending somebody a box of OBA just because.

Do you ship outside California?

Not currently. OBA Cookies is keeping shipping within California at this time.
