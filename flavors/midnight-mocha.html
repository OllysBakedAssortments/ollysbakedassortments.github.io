Meet The Cookie

Dark, Fudgy, and Made for Chocolate Lovers.

Midnight Mocha leans all the way into chocolate. A fudgy double chocolate brownie-style cookie brings a deep cocoa base, while dark chocolate chunks and milk chocolate chips add pockets of rich, melty sweetness throughout.

A hint of espresso rounds everything out without turning it into a coffee cookie — it simply makes the chocolate taste darker, richer, and more intense. It's an OBA classic for anyone who likes their cookies on the decadent side.
