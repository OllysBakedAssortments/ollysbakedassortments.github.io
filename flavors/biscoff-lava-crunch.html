Meet The Cookie

Cookie Butter, Turned All the Way Up.

Biscoff Lava Crunch takes the warm, caramelized flavor of Biscoff and builds it into a soft, bakery-style cookie packed with cookie-butter goodness. The center is stuffed with creamy Biscoff spread for a rich, molten bite.

A Biscoff drizzle, butterscotch chips, and crunchy cookie crumbles finish the top, giving every bite a mix of soft cookie, creamy filling, and crisp spiced-cookie crunch.
