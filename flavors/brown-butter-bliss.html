Meet The Cookie

A Classic With a Little Extra Bliss.

Brown Butter Bliss takes the familiar comfort of a chocolate chip cookie and gives it the OBA treatment. Rich brown butter brings a deeper, toasted flavor to every bite while plenty of chocolate keeps things melty, rich, and familiar.

The result is a cookie with crisp golden edges, a soft center, and plenty of chocolate throughout — the kind of classic that earned itself a permanent place in the OBA flavor collection.
